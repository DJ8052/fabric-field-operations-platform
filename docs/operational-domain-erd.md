# Operational Domain Entity Relationship Diagram

**Phase 9 — Operational Domain Design**

## Purpose

This document describes the logical relationships between the operational source entities.

The ERD represents the operational source model only.

It does not represent the Gold dimensional model.

## Central Design Principle

Field Schedule is the operational hub.

It connects:

- Project
- Job Site
- Crew
- Activity

Other entities support those operational events through relationships rather than a sequential chain.

## Primary Supporting Entities

- Region
- Office
- Employee
- Equipment Type
- Equipment
- Equipment Assignment
- Safety Threshold
