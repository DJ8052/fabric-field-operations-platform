# Risk Engine Specification

**Phase 9 — Operational Domain Design**

## Purpose

This document defines how operational weather risk is evaluated.

The Risk Engine combines:

- Weather forecasts
- Operational schedules
- Safety thresholds
- Equipment exposure
- Business rules

to produce consistent operational recommendations.

## Design Principles

- Deterministic
- Explainable
- Versioned
- Auditable

## Outputs

The engine will produce:

- Risk score
- Final risk level
- Recommendation
- Explanation
- Model version
