# Risk Validation Matrix

**Phase 9 — Operational Domain Design**

## Purpose

This document verifies that the Risk Engine produces the expected results for representative operational scenarios.

Unlike the Dashboard Coverage Matrix, this document validates scoring logic rather than reporting coverage.

## Validation Categories

- Normal operations
- Moderate weather exposure
- High wind
- Extreme heat
- Heavy precipitation
- Lightning override
- Equipment exposure
- Crew rescheduling
- Multiple-project conflicts

## Status

Additional validation scenarios will be documented as the Risk Engine is implemented.
